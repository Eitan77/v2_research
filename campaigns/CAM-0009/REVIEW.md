# Campaign Review: CAM-0009

## Decision

Retire CAM-0009 for Type A. The faithful rapid peer-propagation hypothesis
failed at every 5/15/30/60-minute horizon. A slower unhedged sector drift is
real enough to survive broad neighbors and contributor removals, but it is
only a development lead.

The corrected balanced profile averaged 4.21% per recent month at 5 bp per
side, with 10.71% drawdown, 38-day recovery, and 11 positive versus four
negative months. Every leader and peer removal stayed positive.

## Why it is not Type A

April 2025 contributed 29.73%, August lost 9.05%, and excluding the best month
cuts the recent average to 2.39%. At 10 bp, the recent-12-month average falls
to 0.37%. Half-dollar SMH hedging produces a negative recent-12-month result;
dollar- and beta-neutral expressions lose. Only two of 20,000 bootstrap paths
reach a 13% average month.

## Coverage

The campaign covered raw/QQQ/SMH residuals, both signs, protected shorts,
1/5/10/15/30-minute formations, 30/60/120/close exits, entry delays,
shock/attention/liquidity thresholds, cooldowns, lag states, one/three peers,
long stops, 2/5/10 bp costs, 10%-50% signal caps, SMH hedges, causal market
states, exact capacity-aware removals, and moving-block adversity.

No quote replay was performed because execution work cannot create the missing
Type-A alpha. The May 1, 2026 holdout remains untouched.

## Three perspectives

**Researcher:** Semiconductor shocks precede a slower same-session sector move.

**Skeptic:** The return is common-sector-beta sensitive, development-selected,
cost-sensitive, and dominated by one extraordinary month.

**Portfolio engineer:** It may be a conditional lower-return sleeve lead, not
an allocation-ready or Type-A candidate.
