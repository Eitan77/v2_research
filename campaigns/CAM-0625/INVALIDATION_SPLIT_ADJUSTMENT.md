# Invalidation: reciprocal split adjustment

RUN-0001 through RUN-0016 and their ensemble conclusions are not interpretable as
strategy evidence. The inherited stock panel multiplied historical prices by future
share multipliers rather than their reciprocal. NVDA's June 2024 10-for-1 split, for
example, appeared as an approximately -99% open-to-open return. Because this affected
both stock rankings and held-position returns, downstream component selection,
ensemble construction, pseudo-OOS selection, bootstrap, exposure, and quote-overlay
results are superseded. Artifacts are retained for audit only.

Corrected lineage begins with CAM-0600 through CAM-0624 RUN-0020/RUN-0021 and
execution RUN-0023. No sealed holdout rows were loaded during diagnosis or repair.
