# CAM-0631 conclusion audit

The source document was treated as research material, not instructions. The campaign followed its signal-first sequence: event construction, signed future midpoint markout, interpretable chronological toxicity model, simpler-control and cross-asset comparison, frozen untouched-date confirmation, then queue-aware execution.

The signal conclusion is supported but deliberately narrow. The frozen own-book/flow model had positive low-toxicity five-second markout on all twelve confirmation dates, favorable low-versus-high ordering on eleven, and 66.7% pooled symbol breadth. The source-proposed peer residual was non-incremental and was rejected.

The executable conclusion is negative. Same-price trade consumption, displayed queue stress, latency, forced exits, and costs reversed the favorable ex-ante effect. No realistic adverse RUN-0006 configuration was positive. The prespecified RUN-0007 repair executed all 1,296 development rows; none combined at least twenty fills with positive early and late blocks, so the final six dates were not inspected.

Applicable source fidelity, timing, universe, spread, symbol, causal model, queue, cost, short-safety, attrition, reconciliation, and holdout requirements are complete. Weekly/monthly portfolio metrics are inapplicable because the data contract sampled short event windows and no executable portfolio survived. A full-session broker-specific live shadow calibration would be prospective new evidence, not an unfinished historical repair.

Conclusion: historical EPDC execution is rejected under the available evidence. Preserve the toxicity filter as a live-calibration lead only. No paper, live, or profitability claim is authorized.
