# Campaign Review: CAM-0003

## Decision

Retire the recent morning-information to closing-flow continuation mechanism.
Do not promote, forward-test, or spend quote/auction budget. Preserve all five
runs and continue Strategy A in a materially different campaign.

## Evidence

The exact source-positive SPY component lost 9.21% over 18 months and averaged
-0.39% per month over the representative 15 months. Its gross predictive
slope was negative. All 54 overnight/open/timing/confirmation variants lost.

The cross-ETF investigation covered 540 broad, sector, bond, and leveraged
cells plus 270 cells on EEM, FXI, EFA, VWO, and IYR—the ETFs emphasized by the
paper and acquired in 991,191 fresh cutoff-bounded SIP bars. Zero cells in
either grid were positive over the latest 12 months. SOXL's temporary
15-month pocket decayed and had no meaningful unlevered SMH counterpart.

The final source-completion test found no missing leg. All positive-magnitude
tiers lost. Source-direction SPY shorts after negative mornings, protected by
0.5%-2% stops and forced flat before the close, lost 9.93%-10.74%.

## Diagnosis

The historical continuation relationship is inverted in the recent sample:
positive mornings tend to have negative closing returns and negative mornings
tend to have positive closing returns. Timing migration, pre-close
confirmation, opening activity, ETF choice, leverage, and signal magnitude do
not restore positive underlying alpha. Costs worsen the result but do not
cause the negative gross sign.

The campaign's best adapted long result still lost 2.39%. There is therefore
no positive return path whose event concentration, leave-one-out behavior, or
prospective activation could support Strategy A. Macro-event filtering would
be a sparse hindsight rescue of a mechanism with the wrong broad gross sign
and cannot overcome the unlevered closing-window payoff bound.

## Three perspectives

**Researcher:** The paper was faithfully reproduced and its strongest ETF and
state implications were tested; the relationship appears decayed or reversed.

**Skeptic:** No cell is close to the objective, every latest-12-month ETF cell
loses, and the only visible leveraged pocket lacks base alpha.

**Portfolio engineer:** Execution refinement cannot repair negative gross
expectancy. Allocate nothing and spend no quote budget.

The sealed interval beginning 2026-05-01 was never accessed. This retires only
CAM-0003, not the broader Strategy A profile.
