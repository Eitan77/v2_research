# Campaign Review: CAM-0001

## Decision

Development is complete, but the strategy is **not promoted** and is not an
exceptional A-type candidate. Freeze the RUN-0008 specification only for
execution-data acquisition and prospective paper confirmation. Do not reopen
its parameters based on either confirmation sample.

## What survived

The credible finding is a recent long-only continuation state in TQQQ and SOXL:
positive five-session fund momentum, QQQ above a rising SMA20, equal fixed-base
weights, next-open entry, and an open exit ten sessions later. It produced
126.63% net simple P&L over the favorable final 12 months, or 10.55% per month,
with three negative months, 12.85% drawdown, and 85-day recovery. At the neutral
15-month view it produced 128.88%, or 8.59% per month, with three negative and
three zero months and 12.70% drawdown. The 18-month view fell to 6.13% per
month with five negative months and 34.02% drawdown.

The 15-month view is the honest representative denominator. It was predeclared
as neutral, and no causal regime-onset observable justified discarding
February-April 2025. The final 12-month result ranked at the 98.7th percentile
of 77 rolling endpoints, so using it alone would overstate repeatability.

The mechanism has evidence beyond one leveraged ticker. An unleveraged QQQ/SMH
expression preserved the recent path at roughly one-third the return. The exact
candidate retained 10.08% average monthly return at 20 bps per side. SIP
minute-bar fills at 0, 1, 5, and 30 minutes retained 10.38%-10.55%, although a
full extra-session delay fell to 8.95% and extended recovery to 176 days.

## Why it is not exceptional

The result is episodic. Only 19 independent decisions occurred in the final
12 months; the top decision supplied 25.6% and the top five 87.0% of net.
November, December, and February were negative and March was zero. The
full-history path included a losing 2022 and severe 2024H2 failure; full
drawdown was 25.83% and maximum recovery 434 days. Product leverage and active
market beta of 3.21 supplied much of the magnitude.

Selection burden is material. The candidate was adapted after 171 meaningful
variants before RUN-0008. Its event bootstrap was favorable but cannot correct
that search or persistent regime uncertainty. Stops, invalidation exits,
volatility sizing, contributor caps, stricter filters, inverse legs, regime
switches, and causal trailing-P&L suspension rules did not fix recovery and
concentration without sacrificing the return.

Execution is still unqualified. SIP minute-bar latency was robust, but the local
archive contained zero of 45 required TQQQ/SOXL SIP quote symbol-dates.
Historical ETF NBBO could not be fetched because the configured Alpaca client
had no credentials in this session. Spread, displayed size, quote conditions,
and dollar capacity therefore remain unknown. Adverse one/five-minute
entry-high and exit-low bounds fell to 9.34%-9.72% monthly and four negative
months.

## Three-perspective audit

**Researcher:** The cross-asset and parameter-neighbor evidence supports a real
recent bullish continuation state, but the return magnitude is inseparable
from leveraged products and the favorable endpoint.

**Skeptic:** The 15-month return misses the reference objective, recovery is
almost three months, decisions are concentrated, and an adapted 98.7th
percentile endpoint is not independent evidence. Quote qualification is absent.

**Portfolio engineer:** Gross exposure is capped at the original 1.0 capital
base, but embedded leverage, beta, gap exposure, and unresolved drawdowns make
this unsuitable as dependable monthly income. It may merit a small paper
shadow, not allocation.

## Prospective contract

If independently confirmed, activation is exactly the frozen QQQ-above-and-
rising-SMA20 plus positive five-session fund momentum rule. Monitor rolling
three- and six-month fixed-rule net expectancy, positive-month frequency,
drawdown/recovery, contributor breadth, execution slippage, and parameter
neighbors. Suspend—not tune—if both rolling expectancy views are nonpositive,
or if live drawdown/recovery materially breaches development experience, or if
realized round-trip execution erodes the severe-cost margin. Retire only after
persistent prospective failure plus a mechanism review.

The sealed interval beginning 2026-05-01 was not accessed. The broader Strategy
A profile is not exhausted; only this leveraged directional-persistence
campaign has no remaining principled in-sample adaptation.
